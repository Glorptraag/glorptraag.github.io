Class A & B Licensed

Asbestos Testing & Removal — Our 8-Step Process

As an approved Class A & B Asbestos Removal company, we perform asbestos-related work across commercial and residential buildings, friable burn-down demolitions, and commercial asset remediation. Every step documented. Every clearance certified.

01

Inspection & Testing

Licensed inspector examines the building. Non-friable and friable samples lab-tested to confirm asbestos content.

02

Planning & Permits

Removal plan created to regulation. Equipment, disposal, worker protection defined. All permits obtained from authorities.

03

Site Preparation

Work area sealed — plastic sheeting, negative air pressure. HVAC isolated. Restricted access zones established.

04

Worker Protection

Full PPE: disposable coveralls, gloves, boots, P100 respirators. Decontamination unit for safe entry/exit.

05

Wet Removal

ACM materials wet-sprayed. Removed carefully into leak-tight, labelled containers or double-bagged immediately.

06

Cleanup & Decon

HEPA-vacuumed, wet-wiped. All tools cleaned. Waste sealed, labelled, transported to approved hazardous waste facility.

07

Air Clearance Testing

Certified inspector performs air monitoring. Once cleared, containment barriers safely removed.

08

Documentation

Waste manifests, air clearance results, work logs and certifications — full project record delivered to you.
