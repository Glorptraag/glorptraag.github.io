Asbestos Roof Removal

Protect your home with safe asbestos roof removal

If your roof was built before the 1990s, there’s a strong chance it contains asbestos. Asbestos roofing was once common across Queensland, but over time, it has become one of the biggest health concerns for property owners. At Coastal Demolitions, we help you take care of that risk through professional, fully licensed asbestos roof removal.

As one of the trusted asbestos roof removal companies on the Gold Coast and trusted across the Sunshine Coast and Brisbane, we work with property owners and builders to safely remove roofing materials that may contain asbestos.

Know what to expect with our roof asbestos removal process

We follow a structured and compliant process to keep your household and business safe during removal.

1. Inspection and consultation

Every job starts with a detailed site inspection to confirm if asbestos is present. We assess the condition of the roofing materials and provide a free quote.

2. Planning and preparation

We prepare a removal plan based on site conditions, safety requirements and disposal needs. You’ll know exactly what the process involves before any work begins.

3. Asbestos removal from roof

Our licensed team uses strict safety controls to remove asbestos without causing harmful fibres to become airborne. We work with minimal disruption to your home or site.

4. Disposal and decontamination

Removed material is contained securely and transported in registered vehicles to approved asbestos waste facilities.

5. Clearance and certification

Once the job is complete, we conduct a final site check and issue a clearance certificate to confirm it’s safe for re-entry.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can asbestos be found in places other than the roof?

Yes. Asbestos was commonly used in various parts of older homes, including bathrooms, walls and flooring. If you’re concerned about bathroom materials, check out our Asbestos Bathroom Removal Guide for helpful tips on identifying signs and understanding the next steps.

Can I remove asbestos from my roof myself?

No. In Australia, roof asbestos removal must be carried out by licensed professionals due to the serious health risks involved. DIY removal is not only dangerous but also illegal without proper accreditation.

Do you provide roof asbestos removal for both homes and commercial buildings?

Yes. Coastal Demolitions handles roof asbestos removal for a wide range of residential and commercial properties, with tailored methods to suit each project.
